# The Hero Canon

## Human accountability in the age of abundant intelligence

AI can generate more than organizations can responsibly absorb.

The Hero Canon is a bilingual organizational manifesto, theory, constitution, and case-based learning system for accountable value creation. It is written for founders, executives, product leaders, architects, engineers, and institutions designing human responsibility in an AI-native world.

> Intelligence may become abundant. Responsibility does not disappear.

## Published Canon

- **Canon 000 — Hero Brand Book v1.1:** identity, language, architecture, and publication baseline.
- **Canon 001 — The Hero Manifesto v1.1:** the declaration for human accountability in the Age of Abundant Intelligence.
- **Canon 002 — Hero Foundation v1.1:** the theory, boundaries, counterclaims, and research agenda for accountable value creation.
- **Canon 003 — Hero Constitution v1.0:** durable principles, rights, responsibilities, prohibitions, and legitimacy boundaries.
- **Canon 008 — Hero Casebook v1.0:** causal diagnosis and prevention conditions derived from sourced real cases.
